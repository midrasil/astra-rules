# Character Creation

**Character Creation** is the process of building a new character in Astra. The result is a sheet ready to play, with Attributes, Skills, Privileges, Competencies, Complications and starting equipment.

The steps are done in order, but you can go back and adjust earlier choices while the character's concept takes shape.

## Subsections

- [Identity](1%20-%20Identidade.md) — Concept, Ambition, Desire and Convictions.
- [Attributes and Skills](2%20-%20Atributos%20e%20Per%C3%ADcias.md) — Attributes (fixed array) and Skills (profiles).
- [Derived Traits](3%20-%20Caracter%C3%ADsticas%20Derivadas.md) — Health, Willpower, Initiative and Maximum Load.
- [Privileges and Complications](4%20-%20Privilégios%20e%20Complicações.md) — 7 Privilege Points and 4 Complication Points.
- [Competencies](5%20-%20Competências.md) — 2 Competency Points.
- [Starting Equipment](6%20-%20Equipamento%20Inicial.md) — what the character starts out carrying.

Finally, round out the character with a name, appearance and backstory. The sheet is ready to play; later adjustments, spending XP, follow the [Advancement](../Regras/Personagens/Progress%C3%A3o.md) rules.
