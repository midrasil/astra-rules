# Attributes and Skills

Beyond the concept, a character is defined numerically by their [Attributes](../Regras/Personagens/Atributos.md) and [Skills](../Regras/Personagens/Per%C3%ADcias.md). This subsection covers the starting distribution of points between them.

## Attributes

Every character has the six core [Attributes](../Regras/Personagens/Atributos.md), with values from **1 to 5** (see [Scale](../Regras/Personagens/Atributos.md#scale)).

Distribute the values below among the six Attributes, choosing **where each one goes**:

> **One Attribute at 4 · two at 3 · two at 2 · one at 1**

## Skills

[Skills](../Regras/Personagens/Per%C3%ADcias.md) range from **0 to 5** (see [Scale](../Regras/Personagens/Per%C3%ADcias.md#scale)). Each Skill has a **linked Attribute**, normally used to form its Dice Pool.

The core has **24 Skills**. Start with all of them at **0** and choose **one** of the three profiles:

- **Generalist** — one Skill at 3, six at 2 and six at 1.
- **Balanced** — two at 3, four at 2 and six at 1.
- **Specialist** — one at 4, two at 3, two at 2 and two at 1.

Skills that get no points stay at **0**. Points can also go into a [Specific Knowledge](../Regras/Personagens/Per%C3%ADcias.md#specific-knowledge), and each Level in Scholarship gives an additional [language](../Regras/Personagens/Per%C3%ADcias.md#languages).

## Cap during creation

During creation, no Attribute or Skill goes above **4**. The value **5** is only reached with [XP](../Regras/Personagens/Progress%C3%A3o.md).
