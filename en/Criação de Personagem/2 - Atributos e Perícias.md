# Attributes and Skills

Beyond the concept, a character is defined numerically by their [Attributes](../Regras/Personagens/Atributos.md) and [Skills](../Regras/Personagens/Per%C3%ADcias.md). This subsection covers the starting distribution of points between them.

## Attributes

Every character has the six core [Attributes](../Regras/Personagens/Atributos.md), with values from **1 to 5** (see [Scale](../Regras/Personagens/Atributos.md#scale)).

Distribute the fixed array below, choosing **where each value goes**:

> **One Attribute at 4 · two at 3 · two at 2 · one at 1**

No Attribute can go above **4** during creation — the value **5** is only reached with XP.

## Skills

[Skills](../Regras/Personagens/Per%C3%ADcias.md) range from **0 to 5** (0 = untrained). Each Skill has a **linked Attribute**, normally used to form its Dice Pool.

The core has **24 Skills**, including **Acrobatics**, **Throwing**, **General Knowledge** and **Repair**. Start with all of them at **0** and choose **one** of the three profiles:

- **Generalist** — one skill at 3, six at 2 and seven at 1.
- **Balanced** — two at 3, four at 2 and five at 1.
- **Specialist** — one at 4, two at 3, two at 2 and two at 1.

Skills that get no points stay at **0**. The cap during creation is also **4**.
