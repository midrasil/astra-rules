# Identity

Before and beyond the numbers, a character is defined by **who they are**: their concept and what drives them. This subsection gathers those choices.

## Concept

Define **who the character is** in a few words: origin, occupation, personality and what drives them. The concept guides the following choices — which Attributes to prioritize, which [Privileges](../Regras/Privil%C3%A9gios/Privil%C3%A9gios.md) fit and which [Complications](../Regras/Complica%C3%A7%C3%B5es/Complica%C3%A7%C3%B5es.md) make sense.

## Ambition and Desire

Choose **1 Ambition** and **1 Desire** (see [Ambition and Desire](../Regras/Personagens/Ambi%C3%A7%C3%A3o%20e%20Desejo.md)).

Ambition is a long-term goal; Desire, something immediate.

## Convictions and Pillars

Choose **3 Convictions** and the **Pillar** that sustains each one (see [Convictions and Pillars](../Regras/Personagens/Convic%C3%A7%C3%B5es%20e%20Pilares.md)).

A Conviction is a principle the character refuses to betray; the Pillar, the person, place or object that embodies it.
