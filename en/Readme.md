# Astra

**Astra** is a generic and modular tabletop RPG system, designed to serve as a base for different settings, genres and play styles.

Its structure takes as its main reference the systems of the **Storyteller** family, especially their most recent editions, using dice pools and an approach centered on characters, conflicts and consequences. At the same time, it brings in concepts inspired by systems such as **Savage Worlds Adventure Edition (SWADE)**, **Pathfinder** and **Dungeons & Dragons**, mainly in aspects related to modularity, character customization and tactical combat.

Astra was conceived with a philosophy similar to that of an open source software project: there is a consistent rules core, on top of which different modules, adaptations and settings can be built without all of them having to use exactly the same tools.

The system is tightly integrated with **Foundry Virtual Tabletop**, which works as its main digital implementation. The goal, however, is not to limit Astra to the virtual table: its rules are designed so they can be used both at in-person tables and on other platforms.

## System rules

The core is organized into eleven main areas:

- [Basic Mechanics](Regras/Mec%C3%A2nica%20B%C3%A1sica/Mec%C3%A2nica%20B%C3%A1sica.md) — resolving tests and results.
- [Characters](Regras/Personagens/Personagens.md) — the characters' capabilities, resources, motivations and traits.
- [Privileges](Regras/Privil%C3%A9gios/Privil%C3%A9gios.md) — the character's relationships, recognition, social standing, properties and resources.
- [Competencies](Regras/Compet%C3%AAncias/Compet%C3%AAncias.md) — sets of Capabilities that change what the character can do.
- [Complications](Regras/Complica%C3%A7%C3%B5es/Complica%C3%A7%C3%B5es.md) — narrative problems that create situations and consequences in the story.
- [Creatures](Regras/Criaturas/Criaturas.md) — creature types and traits specific to NPCs.
- [Scenes and Conflicts](Regras/Cenas%20e%20Conflitos/Cenas%20e%20Conflitos.md) — structure of tactical scenes, turns and confrontation actions.
- [Moving](Regras/Movimento/Movimento.md) — getting around and actions related to movement.
- [Equipment](Regras/Equipamentos/Equipamentos.md) — load, common items, weapons, armor, shields and switching items.
- [Resistance and Vulnerability](Regras/Resist%C3%AAncia%20e%20Vulnerabilidade/Resist%C3%AAncia%20e%20Vulnerabilidade.md) — how characters and creatures react to damage types.
- [Conditions](Regras/Condi%C3%A7%C3%B5es/Condi%C3%A7%C3%B5es.md) — states that affect characters during a scene.
