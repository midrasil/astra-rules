# Parrier

Parrier represents the character trained to turn blows aside with their own weapon, using its blade, haft or weight to block an Attack instead of relying on a shield.

The Competency has two Levels. Level 1 grants the Parry Capability, and Level 2 broadens the Attacks that can be parried.

## Progression

| Level | Capability granted |
|---:|---|
| **1** | Parry |
| **2** | Parry Projectiles |

## Level 1 — Parry

You can choose to [Defend](../../Cenas%20e%20Conflitos/A%C3%A7%C3%B5es/Confrontar.md#defend) against a melee Attack using a melee weapon you are holding, even without a Shield.

When Parrying, instead of the normal Defend Pool, you use the same Pool as a melee [Attack](../../Cenas%20e%20Conflitos/A%C3%A7%C3%B5es/Confrontar.md) with the weapon used and, in place of the Shield's Protection, add the **weapon's Damage**:

> **Parry Pool = the weapon's Attack Pool + weapon Damage**

By default, this is **Agility + Fighting + weapon Damage**. The same rules that change the Pool of an Attack with the weapon apply to Parrying: a [Heavy](../../Equipamentos/Armas.md#use-optional) weapon uses **Strength + Fighting + weapon Damage**, and a Capability that sets another Attribute or Pool for Attacks with the weapon also takes precedence here.

Bonuses and penalties that depend on starting an Attack, such as [Numerical Advantage](../../Cenas%20e%20Conflitos/A%C3%A7%C3%B5es/Confrontar.md#numerical-advantage), do not apply to Parrying.

The Damage counted is the weapon's Damage at the time of the Confront, including the +1 of a [Flexible](../../Equipamentos/Armas.md#grip-optional) weapon held in both hands. The weapon's Special Properties are not triggered when Parrying.

The rest of the resolution follows the normal Defend rules: if you win, the Attack is blocked and no one takes damage; on a tie, the attacker deals only their weapon's Damage as superficial damage; if you lose, you take the Margin of Successes plus the attacker's weapon Damage.

If you are [fighting with two weapons](../../Cenas%20e%20Conflitos/A%C3%A7%C3%B5es/Confrontar.md#two-weapon-fighting), you choose which of them you use to Parry. The Pool and the Damage added are those of the chosen weapon, and the Damage of the two weapons is not added together.

If you are also equipped with a Shield, you choose when Defending whether to use the Shield's Protection or the weapon's Damage. The two bonuses do not stack.

[Unarmed](../../Equipamentos/Armas.md#unarmed) and other Natural weapons cannot be used to Parry, unless another Capability allows it.

## Level 2 — Parry Projectiles

Your [Parry](#level-1--parry) evolves. You can also Parry ranged Attacks, including shots and thrown weapons, following the same rules as Level 1.

You still need to hold a melee weapon to Parry, and the Pool is still the weapon's melee Attack Pool plus the weapon's Damage.
