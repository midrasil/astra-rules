# Acrobat

Acrobat represents the character who slips past blows, crosses the battlefield and refuses to be surrounded.

## Progression

| Level | Capability granted | Light Feet |
|---:|---|---:|
| **1** | Acrobatic Dodge | — |
| **2** | Light Feet | +2 |
| **3** | Hard to Surround | +3 |
| **4** | Evasion | +4 |
| **5** | Untouchable | +5 |

## Level 1 — Acrobatic Dodge

When you win a [Dodge](../../Cenas%20e%20Conflitos/A%C3%A7%C3%B5es/Confrontar.md#dodge), you can move up to your **full base Movement**, instead of half of it.

During this movement, you can pass through squares occupied by enemies, but you cannot end it in one of them.

## Level 2 — Light Feet

Your base Movement increases by a number of squares equal to your **Acrobat Level**. The increase also applies to [Run](../../Movimento/Correr.md) and to the movement when Dodging.

In addition:

- [Difficult Terrain](../../Movimento/Movimenta%C3%A7%C3%A3o.md#difficult-terrain) costs only **1 Movement point** per square for you. [Special Movement](../../Movimento/Movimenta%C3%A7%C3%A3o.md#special-movement) still costs 2 points;
- standing up from [Prone](../../Condi%C3%A7%C3%B5es/Ca%C3%ADdo.md) costs only **1 Movement point**. With Movement 0, you still cannot stand up.

## Level 3 — Hard to Surround

Attackers do not get [Numerical Advantage](../../Cenas%20e%20Conflitos/A%C3%A7%C3%B5es/Confrontar.md#numerical-advantage) against you. You still count as usual toward your allies' Numerical Advantage.

During your Movement, you can pass through squares occupied by enemies. Each one costs **1 extra Movement point**, and you cannot end your movement in one of them.

These effects do not work while you are unable to move.

## Level 4 — Evasion

When resisting a [Damage Zone](../../Cenas%20e%20Conflitos/Zonas.md#damage-zones), if you get Successes **equal to or greater than the Zone's Damage** and can leave the area with the movement of a won Dodge, you move out of the Zone and **take no damage**. The movement must end outside the Zone's area.

If you do not get enough Successes, cannot leave the area or choose not to move, resisting the Zone is resolved as usual.

## Level 5 — Untouchable

When Dodging, you win on a tie: you avoid all damage and can make the [Acrobatic Dodge](#level-1--acrobatic-dodge) movement.
