# Slayer

Slayer represents the character who hunts the creatures the ordinary world fears and does not understand: the dead that do not rest, spirits and aberrations. They recognize the signs those creatures leave, know their weaknesses and endure what they do to others in order to put an end to them for good.

This Competency's Capabilities use the [Occultism](../Per%C3%ADcias.md#list-of-skills) Skill.

A **monster** is a creature of the **Undead**, **Spirit** or **Aberration** [Type](../Tipos%20de%20Criatura.md).

## Progression

| Level | Capability granted | Weak Spot |
|---:|---|---:|
| **1** | Signs of the Supernatural | — |
| **2** | Know the Monster | — |
| **3** | Mutant Blood | — |
| **4** | Weak Spot | +4 |
| **5** | Monster Slayer | +5 |

## Level 1 — Signs of the Supernatural

You get **+2 dice** on **Cunning + Occultism** tests to find and interpret the traces left by monsters, such as marks, victims, hauntings and the remains of rituals.

When you successfully interpret traces, you also learn the Type of the creature that left them, how many there are, how long ago they passed and what brought them there.

## Level 2 — Know the Monster

You can use your Action to study a monster you can observe. Test **Cunning + Occultism** against a [Difficulty](../../Mec%C3%A2nica%20B%C3%A1sica/Reserva%20de%20Dados.md#difficulty-scale) set by the Narrator.

Whatever the result, the monster becomes [Read](../../Condi%C3%A7%C3%B5es/Lido.md) by you until the end of the Scene. You cannot study a monster already Read by you.

- **Success:** you ask the Narrator 1 question from the list below, plus 1 for each Success above the Difficulty.
- **Failure:** you find out nothing.

**Questions:**

- What is one of its [Resistances](../../Resist%C3%AAncia%20e%20Vulnerabilidade/Resist%C3%AAncia.md) or [Immunities](../../Resist%C3%AAncia%20e%20Vulnerabilidade/Imunidade.md)?
- What is one of its [Vulnerabilities](../../Resist%C3%AAncia%20e%20Vulnerabilidade/Vulnerabilidade.md)?
- What is one of its [Traits](../Caracter%C3%ADsticas%20de%20NPC.md)?
- What interrupts its [Regeneration](../Caracter%C3%ADsticas%20de%20NPC.md#regeneration-2-interrupted-by-the-damage-type-or-material-set-on-the-creature)?
- What draws it, what drives it away and what binds it to this place?

Each question reveals a single Resistance, Immunity, Vulnerability or Trait. When the monster has none, the Narrator says so.

When interpreting a monster's traces with [Signs of the Supernatural](#level-1--signs-of-the-supernatural), you can make this test from the traces, without spending an Action. The monster becomes Read by you during the first Scene in which you find it.

## Level 3 — Mutant Blood

You get **+2 dice** on tests to resist [Terrifying](../Caracter%C3%ADsticas%20de%20NPC.md#terrifying-potency-2-determination--cunning-stacks-equal-to-potency) and the on-hit effects of any creature: **Venomous**, **Paralyzing**, **Stunning** and **Infectious**.

## Level 4 — Weak Spot

When you attack a monster [Read](../../Condi%C3%A7%C3%B5es/Lido.md) by you with a Damage Type or Material to which it has a [Vulnerability](../../Resist%C3%AAncia%20e%20Vulnerabilidade/Vulnerabilidade.md), the weapon's Damage increases by an amount equal to your **Slayer Level**: **+4** at Level 4 and **+5** at Level 5.

The bonus applies to any kind of Attack, including on a tie, when the weapon's Damage is dealt as superficial damage. It does not add dice to the Pool.

## Level 5 — Monster Slayer

You get **+2 dice** when starting a Confrontation with an Attack against a monster [Read](../../Condi%C3%A7%C3%B5es/Lido.md) by you.

All damage you deal to that monster interrupts its [Regeneration](../Caracter%C3%ADsticas%20de%20NPC.md#regeneration-2-interrupted-by-the-damage-type-or-material-set-on-the-creature), whatever the Damage Type or the weapon's Material.
