# Assassin

Assassin represents the character who wins before the enemy realizes the fight has begun. Outside combat, they use silence and patience to reach the target without being noticed. When the conflict starts, they act first and strike before the opponent has a chance to react.

The Competency is not limited to one kind of weapon. Its Capabilities build on [Initiative](../../Cenas%20e%20Conflitos/A%20Cena.md#initiative), the **Stealth** Skill and the [target without a response](../../Cenas%20e%20Conflitos/A%C3%A7%C3%B5es/Confrontar.md#target-without-a-response) rule.

Level 5 does not grant a new Capability. It increases the potency of Sneak Attack and Silent Steps.

## Progression

| Level | Capability granted | Sneak Attack | Silent Steps |
|---:|---|---:|---:|
| **1** | First Strike | — | — |
| **2** | Sneak Attack | +2 | — |
| **3** | Silent Steps | +3 | +3 dice |
| **4** | Lethal Approach | +4 | +4 dice |
| **5** | — | +5 | +5 dice |

## Level 1 — First Strike

Your [Initiative](../../Cenas%20e%20Conflitos/A%20Cena.md#initiative) Pool can use **Agility** instead of Determination:

> **Initiative Pool = Cunning + Agility**

If the Tactical Scene begins without the enemies having noticed your presence, you also add to your Initiative Pool a number of dice equal to your **Stealth** Level. The Narrator decides whether the enemies had already noticed you, usually based on the Stealth tests made before the Scene.

When you start a Confront against a target whose first Turn in the Scene has not yet come, the target is considered **surprised** and becomes a [target without a response](../../Cenas%20e%20Conflitos/A%C3%A7%C3%B5es/Confrontar.md#target-without-a-response): they do not roll, and their result always counts as **1 Success**.

A target who used [Delay the Turn](../../Cenas%20e%20Conflitos/A%C3%A7%C3%B5es/Aguardar.md#delay-the-turn) is not considered surprised. A participant who joins the Scene after it begins is considered surprised until their first Turn comes.

## Level 2 — Sneak Attack

When you win an Attack against a [target without a response](../../Cenas%20e%20Conflitos/A%C3%A7%C3%B5es/Confrontar.md#target-without-a-response), you add to the damage an amount equal to your **Assassin Level**.

The bonus applies whatever the reason the target cannot respond: surprised by [First Strike](#level-1--first-strike), unconscious, [Paralyzed](../../Condi%C3%A7%C3%B5es/Paralisado.md) or with their Pool reduced below 1 die. It also applies outside a Tactical Scene, when the Narrator determines that the target does not notice the Attack.

The bonus increases automatically as you level up: **+2** at Level 2, **+3** at Level 3, **+4** at Level 4 and **+5** at Level 5.

The bonus adds to the weapon's Damage, applies to any kind of Attack, whether melee, shooting or thrown, and does not add dice to the Pool. It does not apply on a tie, when the attacker deals only the weapon's Damage as superficial damage.

## Level 3 — Silent Steps

You get a number of extra dice equal to your Assassin Level on every **Stealth** and **Sleight of Hand** test.

This bonus does not apply to the Initiative Pool. When the Tactical Scene begins without the enemies having noticed you, [First Strike](#level-1--first-strike) adds only your Stealth Level to Initiative, without the extra dice from this Capability.

The bonus increases automatically as you level up: **+3 dice** at Level 3, **+4** at Level 4 and **+5** at Level 5.

## Level 4 — Lethal Approach

On your first Turn of each Tactical Scene, you get the effect of the [Run](../../Movimento/Correr.md) Action without spending your Action.
