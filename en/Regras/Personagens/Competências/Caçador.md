# Hunter

Hunter represents the character who lives off the wilds and the creatures that dwell in them. They follow tracks others cannot see, know the animals, monsters and supernatural creatures they pursue in depth and use that knowledge to bring them down.

This Competency's Capabilities use the **Survival** Skill, the **Nature** [Specific Knowledge](../Per%C3%ADcias.md#specific-knowledge): in-depth knowledge of the fauna, flora, terrain and climate of the wilds; and the [Occultism](../Per%C3%ADcias.md#list-of-skills) Skill. Without a Level in Nature or Occultism, the Pool uses Cunning alone.

A **prey** is a creature of any [Type](../Tipos%20de%20Criatura.md) except **Humanoid**. Against Beast, Plant, Monstrosity and Dragon, the study uses **Nature**; against Undead, Construct, Elemental, Spirit and Aberration, it uses **Occultism**.

## Progression

| Level | Capability granted | Weak Spot |
|---:|---|---:|
| **1** | Tracker | — |
| **2** | Know the Prey | — |
| **3** | Hunter's Strike | — |
| **4** | Weak Spot | +4 |
| **5** | Relentless Hunt | +5 |

## Level 1 — Tracker

You get **+2 dice** on **Survival** tests to follow tracks and to erase your group's tracks.

When you successfully follow tracks, you also learn which creature left them, how many there are, how long ago they passed and whether they were wounded or in a hurry.

## Level 2 — Know the Prey

You can use your Action to study a prey you can observe. Test **Cunning + Nature** or **Cunning + Occultism**, depending on the prey's Type, against a [Difficulty](../../Mec%C3%A2nica%20B%C3%A1sica/Reserva%20de%20Dados.md#difficulty-scale) set by the Narrator.

Whatever the result, the prey becomes [Read](../../Condi%C3%A7%C3%B5es/Lido.md) by you until the end of the Scene. You cannot study a prey already Read by you.

- **Success:** you ask the Narrator 1 question from the list below, plus 1 for each Success above the Difficulty.
- **Failure:** you find out nothing.

**Questions:**

- What is one of its [Resistances](../../Resist%C3%AAncia%20e%20Vulnerabilidade/Resist%C3%AAncia.md) or [Immunities](../../Resist%C3%AAncia%20e%20Vulnerabilidade/Imunidade.md)?
- What is one of its [Vulnerabilities](../../Resist%C3%AAncia%20e%20Vulnerabilidade/Vulnerabilidade.md)?
- What is one of its [Traits](../Caracter%C3%ADsticas%20de%20NPC.md)?
- How does it hunt, attack or defend itself?
- Is it wounded, hungry or cornered?

Each question reveals a single Resistance, Immunity, Vulnerability or Trait. When the prey has none, the Narrator says so.

When following a prey's tracks with [Tracker](#level-1--tracker), you can make this test from the tracks, without spending an Action. The prey becomes Read by you during the first Scene in which you find it.

## Level 3 — Hunter's Strike

You get **+2 dice** when starting a Confrontation with an Attack against a prey [Read](../../Condi%C3%A7%C3%B5es/Lido.md) by you.

## Level 4 — Weak Spot

When you attack a prey [Read](../../Condi%C3%A7%C3%B5es/Lido.md) by you with a Damage Type or Material to which it has a [Vulnerability](../../Resist%C3%AAncia%20e%20Vulnerabilidade/Vulnerabilidade.md), the weapon's Damage increases by an amount equal to your **Hunter Level**: **+4** at Level 4 and **+5** at Level 5.

The bonus applies to any kind of Attack, including on a tie, when the weapon's Damage is dealt as superficial damage. It does not add dice to the Pool.

## Level 5 — Relentless Hunt

When you study a prey, you can mark it as **your hunt**. It remains [Read](../../Condi%C3%A7%C3%B5es/Lido.md) by you across Scenes until it dies or you mark another. You can only have one hunt at a time.

You always know in which direction your hunt is, as long as it is in the wilds and within a day's travel of you.
