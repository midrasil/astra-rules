# Ambidextrous

Ambidextrous represents the fighter who wields a weapon in each hand and uses both in the same strike: while one blade opens the enemy's guard, the other lands the blow.

Under the [Two-Weapon Fighting](../../Cenas%20e%20Conflitos/A%C3%A7%C3%B5es/Confrontar.md#two-weapon-fighting) rules, a character holding two weapons only chooses which one to use on each Confront. The Capabilities of this Competency make the second weapon contribute as well.

## Main weapon and off-hand weapon

The Capabilities of this Competency require a melee weapon held in each hand. On each Confront, you choose which of them is the **main weapon** and which is the **off-hand weapon**:

- the **main weapon** is the weapon of the Attack: it sets the Pool, the Damage, the Damage Type and the Special Properties applied;
- the **off-hand weapon** must have the [Light](../../Equipamentos/Armas.md#grip-optional) Property and be able to reach the target. It does not trigger its own Special Properties.

A Flexible weapon held in one hand does not get the +1 Damage. [Unarmed](../../Equipamentos/Armas.md#unarmed), Natural weapons and Shields do not count as an off-hand weapon.

## Progression

The Competency has three Levels. Level 1 grants Double Strike, Level 2 extends the bonus to defense and Level 3 widens the weapons that can be the off-hand weapon.

| Level | Capability granted | Off-hand weapon |
|---:|---|---|
| **1** | Double Strike | Light |
| **2** | Cross Guard | Light |
| **3** | Ambidexterity | Light or one-handed |

## Level 1 — Double Strike

When you start a Confront with a melee Attack or [Accept the Confront](../../Cenas%20e%20Conflitos/A%C3%A7%C3%B5es/Confrontar.md#accept-the-confront) with a melee weapon, you add to the Pool a number of dice equal to the **off-hand weapon's Damage**:

> **Pool = the main weapon's Attack Pool + off-hand weapon Damage, as dice**

The damage dealt is still the main weapon's. An off-hand weapon with Damage 0 adds no dice.

Double Strike does not apply to ranged or thrown Attacks.

## Level 2 — Cross Guard

When you [Dodge](../../Cenas%20e%20Conflitos/A%C3%A7%C3%B5es/Confrontar.md#dodge) or [Parry](Aparador.md#level-1--parry) a melee Attack, you also add the off-hand weapon's Damage to the Pool as dice.

When Parrying, the main weapon is the one used to Parry and adds its own Damage normally. The off-hand weapon's Damage comes in as dice, as an exception to the Parrier rule that the Damage of the two weapons is not added together.

## Level 3 — Ambidexterity

The off-hand weapon no longer needs to be Light. Any melee weapon held in one hand can be the off-hand weapon. Two-handed weapons still cannot.
