# Characters

This section gathers the elements that define a character's capabilities, resources, motivations and particularities.

## Character creation

- [Character Creation](../../Cria%C3%A7%C3%A3o%20de%20Personagem/0%20-%20Cria%C3%A7%C3%A3o%20de%20Personagem.md) — how to build a character from scratch.

## Abilities

- [Attributes](Atributos.md) — fundamental physical and spiritual abilities.
- [Skills](Per%C3%ADcias.md) — knowledge and abilities the character has developed.

## Nature

- [Size](Tamanho.md) — physical build, space taken up on the map and who can move whom.

## Resources

- [Health](Vida.md) — physical condition, damage and recovery.

## Identity and customization

- [Ambition and Desire](Ambi%C3%A7%C3%A3o%20e%20Desejo.md) — long-term and short-term goals.
- [Convictions and Pillars](Convic%C3%A7%C3%B5es%20e%20Pilares.md) — principles the character upholds and what sustains them.
- [Competencies](../Compet%C3%AAncias/Compet%C3%AAncias.md) — sets of Capabilities that change what the character can do.

## Advancement

- [Advancement](Progress%C3%A3o.md) — earning and spending XP during the campaign.
