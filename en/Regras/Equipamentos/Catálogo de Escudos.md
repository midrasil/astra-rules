# Shield Catalog

This is the shield catalog of the Astra core. See [Shields](Escudos.md) for the Protection, Category, Minimum Strength and Properties rules.

**Value** is abstract and has no currency name.

| Shield | Category | Protection | Minimum Strength | Load | Value | Properties |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Buckler | Light | 2 | — | 1 | 5 | Shield Bash |
| Shield | Medium | 3 | 2 | 1 | 10 | Repel |
| Tower Shield | Heavy | 4 | 3 | 2 | 40 | Cover |

A Heavy Shield imposes **-1 die when Attacking**. Being below the shield's Minimum Strength imposes **-2 Movement**, as in the [Shields](Escudos.md#categories) rules.
