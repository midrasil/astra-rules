# Shield Catalog

This is the shield catalog of the Astra core. See [Shields](Escudos.md) for the Protection, Category and Minimum Strength rules.

**Value** is abstract and has no currency name.

| Shield | Category | Protection | Minimum Strength | Load | Value |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Buckler | Light | 1 | — | 1 | 5 |
| Shield | Medium | 2 | 2 | 1 | 10 |
| Tower Shield | Heavy | 3 | 3 | 2 | 40 |

A Heavy Shield imposes **-1 die when Attacking**. Being below the shield's Minimum Strength imposes **-2 Movement**, as in the [Shields](Escudos.md#categories) rules.
