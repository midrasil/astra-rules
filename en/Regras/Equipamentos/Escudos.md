# Shields

**Shields** are equipment used for defense.

The shields available in the core are in the [Shield Catalog](Cat%C3%A1logo%20de%20Escudos.md).

A character equipped with a **Shield** can choose to **Defend** in a Confront (see [Confront](../Cenas%20e%20Conflitos/A%C3%A7%C3%B5es/Confrontar.md)).

Each shield is described by the following fields:

| Field | Description |
|---|---|
| **Name** | the shield's name |
| **Category** | Light, Medium or Heavy (see [Categories](#categories)) |
| **Minimum Strength** | the Strength needed to use the shield without losing Movement |
| **Description** | a short description of the shield |
| **Protection** | bonus granted when Defending |
| **Load** | how many slots the shield takes |
| **Value** | the shield's cost |

## Protection

When choosing to **Defend**, the character adds the shield's **Protection** to their Dice Pool (see [Confront](../Cenas%20e%20Conflitos/A%C3%A7%C3%B5es/Confrontar.md)).

## Categories

| Category | Protection | Minimum Strength | Load | Other cost |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Light** | 1 | — | 1 | — |
| **Medium** | 2 | 2 | 1 | — |
| **Heavy** | 3 | 3 | 2 | -1 die when Attacking |

With Strength below the Minimum Strength of the shield in hand, the character has **-2 Movement** (the same penalty as armor, which does not stack).

A Heavy Shield also imposes **-1 die when Attacking** while it is held.

Capabilities can change how a Shield is used or let its Protection apply in other situations when their effect expressly says so.
