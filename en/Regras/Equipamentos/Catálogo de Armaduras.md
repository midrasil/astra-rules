# Armor Catalog

This is the armor catalog of the Astra core. See [Armor](Armaduras.md) for the Absorption, Category, Minimum Strength and Properties rules.

**Value** is abstract, has no currency name and may have decimals.

| Armor | Category | Absorption | Minimum Strength | Load | Value | Properties |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Padded Armor | Light | 1 | — | 1 | 5 | — |
| Leather Armor | Light | 1 | — | 1 | 10 | — |
| Thick Hides | Light | 1 | — | 1 | 10 | — |
| Studded Leather | Light | 1 | — | 1 | 45 | Slashing Resistance 1 |
| Chain Shirt | Medium | 2 | 2 | 2 | 50 | Electric Vulnerability 1 |
| Scale Mail | Medium | 2 | 2 | 2 | 50 | Piercing Resistance 1, Electric Vulnerability 1 |
| Breastplate | Medium | 3 | 2 | 2 | 400 | Electric Vulnerability 1 |
| Chain Mail | Heavy | 4 | 3 | 3 | 75 | Slashing Resistance 1, Electric Vulnerability 1 |
| Splint Armor | Heavy | 4 | 3 | 4 | 200 | Electric Vulnerability 1 |
| Plate Armor | Heavy | 5 | 3 | 4 | 1500 | Electric Vulnerability 1 |

Medium and Heavy armor reduce the Pool used to Dodge by a number of dice equal to their Absorption. Every metal armor in this catalog has **Electric Vulnerability 1**, unless stated otherwise.
