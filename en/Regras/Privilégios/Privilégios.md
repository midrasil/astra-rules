# Privileges

**Privileges** represent what the character has or stands for in the world, such as relationships, recognition, social standing, property and resources.

Each Privilege establishes a truth about the character and their place in the campaign. Its details should be set together by the player and the Narrator.

## Level

Every Privilege has a **Level from 0 to 5**.

| Level | General meaning |
|---:|---|
| **0** | The character does not have the Privilege. |
| **1** | Limited reach or capacity. |
| **2** | Established reach or capacity. |
| **3** | Significant reach or capacity. |
| **4** | Exceptional reach or capacity. |
| **5** | Extraordinary reach or capacity. |

Each entry in the [List of Privileges](Lista%20de%20Privil%C3%A9gios.md) explains concretely what its Levels represent. The value is recorded directly on the sheet, such as `Fame 2` or `Haven 3`.

A Privilege's Level does not automatically grant extra dice or modifiers. It mainly sets its reach, its quality and what the character can accomplish through it in the fiction.

## Customization

When acquiring a Privilege, the player and the Narrator set the elements needed to connect it to the character and the campaign.

Depending on the Privilege, this can include:

- its origin;
- the people, organizations or communities involved;
- the place or circle where it operates;
- the resources it puts at the character's disposal;
- its narrative limits;
- its relationship with other elements of the sheet.

These details are not Specializations and have no cost of their own. They make the Privilege concrete within the story.

## Specializations

Some Privileges have **Specializations**. They add resources, narrative permissions or specific mechanical effects to the Privilege.

Raising the Level does not grant Specializations automatically. Each Specialization is acquired separately and is recorded with its Privilege.

Specializations cannot be freely created. Only the options presented in each Privilege's entry can be acquired.

## No requirements

Privileges have no requirements, prerequisite chains or mandatory incompatibilities. Their entries do not use the **Requirement** field.

## Character creation

During creation, the character receives **7 Privilege Points**.

Each point can acquire:

- **1 Level** in a Privilege; or
- **1 Specialization** of a Privilege.

For example, `Haven 3` with **Armory** and **Library** uses 5 points.

## Acquiring during the campaign

During the campaign, acquiring the first Level or raising a Privilege by one Level costs **3 XP**.

Each Specialization also costs **3 XP**.

The cost is fixed and does not increase at higher Levels.

## Reduction and loss

Campaign events can reduce a Privilege's Level or make the character lose it entirely.

This change should come from the fiction, be decided by the Narrator according to the situation and be discussed together with the players. It does not happen through an automatic penalty or by spending an abstract resource.

A character who repeatedly uses their Influence in compromising ways, for example, can lose Levels as their position deteriorates, until reaching Level 0.

## Format of a Privilege

Each Privilege uses the following format:

- **Name** — on the sheet, the name comes with the Level acquired, such as **Ally — 2**.
- **Description** — presents the concept, guides the capacities of the different Levels and points out the elements that should be customized.
- **Specializations** — presents the available options, when there are any.
