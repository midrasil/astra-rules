# Inspirer

Inspirer represents a character capable of awakening courage and determination through music, speeches, stories, performance or another form of expression.

## Progression

| Level | Capability granted | Inspiration |
|---:|---|---:|
| **1** | Inspiration | +2 dice |
| **2** | Lift the Spirit | +2 dice |
| **3** | Inspiring Chorus | +2 dice |

## Level 1 — Inspiration

When you [Help](../Mec%C3%A2nica%20B%C3%A1sica/Reserva%20de%20Dados.md#help), the granted bonus increases from **+1 to +2 dice**.

You can Help a creature up to **6 squares** away instead of needing to be adjacent, provided it can perceive you.

## Level 2 — Lift the Spirit

Once per Scene, when you Help a creature through [Inspiration](#level-1--inspiration), you can take **1 point of superficial Willpower damage** to make it recover **1 point of superficial Willpower damage**.

## Level 3 — Inspiring Chorus

When you Help through [Inspiration](#level-1--inspiration), you can take **1 point of superficial Willpower damage** to choose a number of allies equal to your **Charisma**.

Each chosen ally must be within **6 squares** and able to perceive you. They all gain the **+2 dice** from Inspiration on the next test they make before the end of their next Turn.

If you use [Lift the Spirit](#level-2--lift-the-spirit), you take that Capability's cost separately and choose one of the allies to recover Willpower.
