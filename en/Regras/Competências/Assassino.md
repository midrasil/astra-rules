# Assassin

Assassin represents the character who reaches the target unnoticed, acts first and strikes before the opponent can react.

## Progression

| Level | Capability granted | Sneak Attack | Silent Steps |
|---:|---|---:|---:|
| **1** | First Strike | — | — |
| **2** | Sneak Attack | +2 | — |
| **3** | Silent Steps | +3 | +3 dice |
| **4** | Lethal Approach | +4 | +4 dice |
| **5** | — | +5 | +5 dice |

## Level 1 — First Strike

Your [Initiative](../Cenas%20e%20Conflitos/A%20Cena.md#initiative) Pool can use **Agility** instead of Determination:

> **Initiative Pool = Cunning + Agility**

If the Tactical Scene begins without the enemies having noticed your presence, you also add to your Initiative Pool a number of dice equal to your **Stealth** Level. The Narrator decides whether the enemies had already noticed you.

When you start a Confront against a target whose first Turn in the Scene has not yet come, the target is considered **surprised** and becomes a [target without a response](../Cenas%20e%20Conflitos/A%C3%A7%C3%B5es/Confrontar.md#target-without-a-response).

A target who used [Delay the Turn](../Cenas%20e%20Conflitos/A%C3%A7%C3%B5es/Aguardar.md#delay-the-turn) is not considered surprised. A participant who joins the Scene after it begins is considered surprised until their first Turn comes.

## Level 2 — Sneak Attack

When you win an Attack against a [target without a response](../Cenas%20e%20Conflitos/A%C3%A7%C3%B5es/Confrontar.md#target-without-a-response), you add to the damage an amount equal to your **Assassin Level**.

The bonus applies whatever the reason the target cannot respond, including when they were surprised by [First Strike](#level-1--first-strike). It also applies outside a Tactical Scene, when the Narrator determines that the target does not notice the Attack.

The bonus adds to the weapon's Damage, applies to any kind of Attack and does not add dice to the Pool. It does not apply on a tie.

## Level 3 — Silent Steps

You get a number of extra dice equal to your Assassin Level on every **Stealth** and **Sleight of Hand** test.

This bonus does not apply to the Initiative Pool, nor to the Stealth Level added by [First Strike](#level-1--first-strike).

## Level 4 — Lethal Approach

On your first Turn of each Tactical Scene, you get the effect of the [Run](../Movimento/Correr.md) Action without spending your Action.
