# Mentalist

Mentalist represents the character who reads people through trained observation, picking up emotions, intentions, lies and an enemy's strengths and weaknesses.

## Progression

| Level | Capability granted | Nothing Escapes |
|---:|---|---:|
| **1** | Cold Read | — |
| **2** | Combat Read | — |
| **3** | Nothing Escapes | +3 dice |
| **4** | Manipulation | +4 dice |
| **5** | I Knew It | +5 dice |

## Level 1 — Cold Read

You can use your Action to analyze a person or creature you can observe. Test **Cunning + Perception** against the target's **Charisma + Deception**. A target who does not try to hide what they feel or does not know they are being observed is treated as a [target without a response](../Cenas%20e%20Conflitos/A%C3%A7%C3%B5es/Confrontar.md#target-without-a-response) and counts as **1 Success**.

Whatever the result, the target becomes [Read](../Condi%C3%A7%C3%B5es/Lido.md) by you until the end of the Scene. You cannot analyze a target already Read by you.

- **Win:** you ask the Narrator a number of questions from the list below equal to your Margin of Successes.
- **Tie:** you ask 1 question, but the target notices they are being analyzed.
- **Loss:** you find out nothing, and the target notices they are being analyzed.

**Questions about the person:**

- What are they feeling right now?
- What do they want at this moment?
- Are they hiding something? (yes or no)
- What or whom are they afraid of?
- What is one true fact about their life, such as their trade, their origin or a close relationship?

**Questions about strengths and weaknesses:**

- What is one of their [Resistances](../Resist%C3%AAncia%20e%20Vulnerabilidade/Resist%C3%AAncia.md) or [Immunities](../Resist%C3%AAncia%20e%20Vulnerabilidade/Imunidade.md)?
- What is one of their [Vulnerabilities](../Resist%C3%AAncia%20e%20Vulnerabilidade/Vulnerabilidade.md)?

Each question about strengths and weaknesses reveals a single Resistance, Immunity or Vulnerability, with its Damage Type or Material. When the target has none, the Narrator says so.

The Narrator answers truthfully, the way the target's body and behavior reveal it: impressions, not exact thoughts, names or specific secrets. When the target is a player character, their player answers.

## Level 2 — Combat Read

When starting a Confront or Accepting the Confront against a target [Read](../Condi%C3%A7%C3%B5es/Lido.md) by you, you can use **Cunning** instead of the Attribute normally set for the Attack's Pool.

The replacement applies to any kind of Attack and takes precedence over the [Heavy](../Equipamentos/Armas.md#use-optional) property.

## Level 3 — Nothing Escapes

You get a number of extra dice equal to your Mentalist Level on every **Cunning + Perception** test, including [Cold Read](#level-1--cold-read).

## Level 4 — Manipulation

On **Persuasion**, **Intimidation** and **Deception** tests, you get a number of extra dice equal to your **Cunning**.

These dice are a bonus, not a second Attribute: the test still uses the Skill's normal Attribute.

## Level 5 — I Knew It

Before rolling any test whose Pool uses **Cunning** against a target [Read](../Condi%C3%A7%C3%B5es/Lido.md) by you, you can take an amount of superficial Willpower damage limited by your Mentalist Level. This includes Attacks made with [Combat Read](#level-2--combat-read).

For each point taken, 1 die of that test's Pool becomes a **guaranteed die**: it is not rolled and counts as 1 Success.
