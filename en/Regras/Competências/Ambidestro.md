# Ambidextrous

Ambidextrous represents the fighter who uses both weapons in the same strike: while one opens the enemy's guard, the other lands the blow.

The Capabilities of this Competency require a melee weapon held in each hand, following [Two-Weapon Fighting](../Cenas%20e%20Conflitos/A%C3%A7%C3%B5es/Confrontar.md#two-weapon-fighting). The off-hand weapon must have the [Light](../Equipamentos/Armas.md#grip-optional) Property. When attacking or Accepting the Confront, it must also be able to reach the opponent in the Confront.

## Progression

| Level | Capability granted |
|---:|---|
| **1** | Double Strike |
| **2** | Cross Guard |
| **3** | Ambidexterity |

## Level 1 — Double Strike

When you start a Confront with a melee Attack or [Accept the Confront](../Cenas%20e%20Conflitos/A%C3%A7%C3%B5es/Confrontar.md#accept-the-confront) with a melee weapon, you add to the Pool a number of dice equal to the **off-hand weapon's Damage**:

> **Pool = the main weapon's Attack Pool + off-hand weapon Damage**

The damage dealt is still the main weapon's.

## Level 2 — Cross Guard

When you [Dodge](../Cenas%20e%20Conflitos/A%C3%A7%C3%B5es/Confrontar.md#dodge) or [Parry](Aparador.md#level-1--parry) a melee Attack, you also add the off-hand weapon's Damage to the Pool as dice.

When Parrying, the main weapon is the one used to Parry and adds its own Damage normally.

## Level 3 — Ambidexterity

The off-hand weapon can be any one-handed melee weapon, not only a Light weapon. A Flexible weapon counts as a one-handed weapon and uses its one-handed Damage.
