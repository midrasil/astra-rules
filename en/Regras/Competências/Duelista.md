# Duelist

Duelist represents a fighter who uses a weapon in one hand and keeps the other free, facing opponents in individual combat.

You are **in a duel** when only you and the target are adjacent to each other, with no other combatants adjacent to either of you.

## Progression

| Level | Capability granted | Singular Combat | Technical Superiority | Challenge |
|---:|---|---:|---:|---:|
| **1** | Singular Combat | +1 die | — | — |
| **2** | Technical Superiority | +2 dice | −2 dice | — |
| **3** | Challenge | +3 dice | −3 dice | −3 dice |

## Level 1 — Singular Combat

While holding a melee weapon in one hand and keeping the other hand free, you gain a number of extra dice equal to your **Duelist Level** when starting a melee Confront against a target with whom you are in a duel.

## Level 2 — Technical Superiority

While holding a melee weapon in one hand and keeping the other hand free, reduce the Numerical Advantage bonus received on melee Attacks against you by an amount equal to your **Duelist Level**.

This reduction cannot make the bonus negative.

## Level 3 — Challenge

While holding a melee weapon in one hand and keeping the other hand free, you can use your Action to Challenge a creature that can perceive you.

Choose **Intimidation**, **Deception** or **Persuasion** and test **Charisma + the chosen Skill** against the target's **Determination + Cunning**.

If you win, the target becomes [Challenged](../Condi%C3%A7%C3%B5es/Desafiado.md) by you until the end of the Scene.

A Challenged creature takes a dice penalty equal to your **Duelist Level** when starting a Confront against any creature other than you.

The Condition ends if you start a Confront against another creature.
