# Medic

Medic represents the character who keeps the wounded on their feet during the fight, with patches made in the heat of combat, and cares for them more efficiently once it is over.

## Progression

| Level | Capability granted |
|---:|---|
| **1** | Patch Up |
| **2** | Hold Fast |
| **3** | Trained Hands |
| **4** | Expert Patch |
| **5** | Field Hospital |

## Level 1 — Patch Up

In a [Tactical Scene](../Cenas%20e%20Conflitos/Cenas%20T%C3%A1ticas.md), you can use your [Action](../Cenas%20e%20Conflitos/A%C3%A7%C3%A3o.md) to patch up an adjacent creature or yourself, spending 1 use of healing supplies, such as a [Healing Kit](../Equipamentos/Cat%C3%A1logo%20de%20Consum%C3%ADveis.md#other-consumables). Roll **Cunning + [Healing](../Personagens/Per%C3%ADcias.md)** and choose how many Successes to use, from 0 up to the total rolled.

The creature becomes [Patched](../Condi%C3%A7%C3%B5es/Remendado.md), with a number of eased points equal to the chosen Successes. At the end of the Scene, the eased damage comes back and it becomes [Impaired](../Condi%C3%A7%C3%B5es/Prejudicado.md).

Each creature can be Patched once per Scene.

## Level 2 — Hold Fast

While Patched by you, the creature ignores the [Debilitated](../Condi%C3%A7%C3%B5es/Debilitado.md) penalty.

## Level 3 — Trained Hands

Outside a Tactical Scene, when a [Treatment](../Personagens/Vida.md#treatment) you perform has at least 1 Success, it converts **1 more point** of aggravated damage into superficial.

You treat and [Patch Up](#level-1--patch-up) without healing supplies, improvising with whatever is at hand.

## Level 4 — Expert Patch

When a creature you Patched becomes Impaired at the end of the Scene, it receives **half the stacks**, rounded down.

## Level 5 — Field Hospital

Outside a Tactical Scene, your Treatments count as performed in [Proper Facilities](../Personagens/Vida.md#proper-facilities).
