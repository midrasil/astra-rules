# Sentinel

Sentinel represents the fighter who controls the space around them, punishing anyone who tries to escape their weapon's reach and anyone who attacks their allies.

The Capabilities of this Competency require you to be holding a melee weapon and use its Reach. They do not work while you are surprised, [Incapacitated](../Condi%C3%A7%C3%B5es/Incapacitado.md) or [Paralyzed](../Condi%C3%A7%C3%B5es/Paralisado.md).

## Progression

| Level | Capability granted |
|---:|---|
| **1** | Opportunity Attack |
| **2** | Halt |
| **3** | Retaliate |
| **4** | Tireless Vigilance |

## Level 1 — Opportunity Attack

Once per Round, when an enemy creature within your melee weapon's Reach voluntarily moves out of it, you can start a [Confront](../Cenas%20e%20Conflitos/A%C3%A7%C3%B5es/Confrontar.md) against it with that weapon, without spending your Action. The Attack happens before it leaves the Reach. Once it is resolved, the creature continues its Turn normally, if it can.

The target can only respond by [Defending](../Cenas%20e%20Conflitos/A%C3%A7%C3%B5es/Confrontar.md#defend), which includes [Parry](Aparador.md#level-1--parry), or [Dodging](../Cenas%20e%20Conflitos/A%C3%A7%C3%B5es/Confrontar.md#dodge). It cannot Accept the Confront.

Forced movement, such as from a Maneuver, and the movement of a won Dodge do not provoke an Opportunity Attack.

## Level 2 — Halt

When you win an [Opportunity Attack](#level-1--opportunity-attack) other than on a tie, the target's remaining Movement for that Turn becomes **0**. It cannot move any more that Turn, not even by [Running](../Movimento/Correr.md), but it can still use its Action in other ways.

On a tie, the Attack deals the normal tie damage and the target continues moving.

## Level 3 — Retaliate

An [Opportunity Attack](#level-1--opportunity-attack) can also be provoked when an enemy creature within your melee weapon's Reach starts a Confront against one of your allies. You attack after that Confront is resolved.

This use follows the same rules and the same per-Round limit, and [Halt](#level-2--halt) also applies to it.

## Level 4 — Tireless Vigilance

After the first [Opportunity Attack](#level-1--opportunity-attack) in a Round, you can make more by taking **1 point of superficial Willpower damage** for each additional use.

Each movement or Confront provokes at most one Opportunity Attack from you.
