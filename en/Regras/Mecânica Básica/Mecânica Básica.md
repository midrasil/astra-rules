# Basic Mechanics

This section gathers the fundamental rules used to resolve actions in **Astra**.

## Rules

- [Dice Pool](Reserva%20de%20Dados.md) — building pools, rolls, successes and results.

## Rounding

Whenever a rule divides a value and the result is not a whole number, **round down**. For example, half of a Movement of 7 is **3**.

## Relations
