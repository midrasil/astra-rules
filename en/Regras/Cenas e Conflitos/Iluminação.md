# Lighting

The **Lighting** of an area affects rolls that depend on seeing, such as attacking, noticing or searching for something.

The Narrator sets the lighting of each area.

| Lighting | Penalty |
|---|---|
| **Bright** | none |
| **Dim** | -2 dice |
| **Dark** | -4 dice |
| **Pitch black** | -6 dice |

A creature with **Darkvision** ignores, up to its range, the Dim and Dark penalties. Pitch black blocks Darkvision (see [NPC Traits](../Criaturas/Caracter%C3%ADsticas%20de%20NPC.md)).
