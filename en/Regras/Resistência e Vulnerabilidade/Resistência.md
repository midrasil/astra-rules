# Resistance

A **Resistance** is a value tied to a specific [Damage Type](../Equipamentos/Armas.md#damage-types) or [Material](../Equipamentos/Armas.md#material-required-one-per-weapon) that helps convert the matching aggravated damage into superficial damage. It always comes with a value — for example, **Fire Resistance 3** or **Common metal Resistance 2**.

Resistances to the weapon's Damage Type and Material add up with each other and with the [armor's Absorption](../Equipamentos/Armaduras.md#absorption) when calculating [Effective Absorption](Resist%C3%AAncia%20e%20Vulnerabilidade.md#effective-absorption). Each point of Effective Absorption converts one point of aggravated damage into superficial: damage only stays aggravated in the part the absorption does not cover.

Resistances can be granted by equipment, Conditions, origins, creatures, spells or effects.

For now, Resistances to Materials are set directly on characters and creatures; armor grants Resistances only to Damage Types.
