# Immunity

An **Immunity** makes damage of a specific [Damage Type](../Equipamentos/Armas.md#damage-types) or [Material](../Equipamentos/Armas.md#material-required-one-per-weapon) simply **not apply** — the character takes no damage at all from that source.

Unlike [Resistances](Resist%C3%AAncia.md) and [Vulnerabilities](Vulnerabilidade.md), Immunity **has no value**: a character is either immune to a Type or Material, or they are not.

Immunity to the weapon's Damage Type or Material takes precedence over Resistances and Vulnerabilities: all of the damage is ignored before [Effective Absorption](Resist%C3%AAncia%20e%20Vulnerabilidade.md#effective-absorption) is even calculated, even if there is Vulnerability to the other.

For now, Immunities to Materials are set directly on characters and creatures.
