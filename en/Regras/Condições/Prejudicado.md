# Impaired

**Impaired** is a stacking Condition. Each stack gives:

> **-1 die on rolls that use a [Physical Attribute](../Personagens/Atributos.md#categories)**

The penalty adds to that of [Debilitated](Debilitado.md), when the [Health](../Personagens/Vida.md) track is full.

The Condition does not end at the end of the Scene. Each [Treatment](../Personagens/Vida.md#treatment) received removes **1 stack**, in addition to its normal effect, and each completed [Long Rest](../Personagens/Descanso%20Prolongado.md) removes **1 stack**.

Becoming Impaired again adds the stacks to those the character already has.

Impaired is applied by the end of the [Patched](Remendado.md) Condition and can be applied by other Capabilities or effects that refer to this Condition.
