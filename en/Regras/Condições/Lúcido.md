# Lucid

A **Lucid** character has a clear mind. The next [Willpower](../Personagens/Vontade.md#using-willpower) use they make **costs no Willpower damage**. After that use, the Condition ends.

If it is not used, the Condition lasts as long as its source says.

Lucid can be applied by [Drugs](../Equipamentos/Consum%C3%ADveis.md#drugs), Capabilities or other effects that refer to this Condition.
