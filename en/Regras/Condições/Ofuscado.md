# Dazzled

For a **Dazzled** character, strong light hurts: **Bright** counts as **Dim** for them (see [Lighting](../Cenas%20e%20Conflitos/Ilumina%C3%A7%C3%A3o.md)).

The Condition lasts as long as its source says. Dazzled can be applied by [Drugs](../Equipamentos/Consum%C3%ADveis.md#drugs), Capabilities or other effects that refer to this Condition.
