# Relentless

A **Relentless** character refuses to fall. While the Condition lasts, they **do not become [Incapacitated](Incapacitado.md)**, even with their whole [Health](../Personagens/Vida.md) track filled with aggravated damage. They are still [Debilitated](Debilitado.md) with a full track.

When the Condition ends, if the track is entirely aggravated, the character becomes Incapacitated.

The Condition lasts as long as its source says. Relentless can be applied by [Drugs](../Equipamentos/Consum%C3%ADveis.md#drugs), Capabilities or other effects that refer to this Condition.
