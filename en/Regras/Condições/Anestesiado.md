# Numbed

A **Numbed** character feels no pain. They ignore the dice penalties of:

- [Debilitated](Debilitado.md);
- [Impaired](Prejudicado.md);
- [Poisoned](Envenenado.md).

The Condition lasts as long as its source says. It does not remove those Conditions: when Numbed ends, the penalties apply again.

Numbed can be applied by [Drugs](../Equipamentos/Consum%C3%ADveis.md#drugs), Capabilities or other effects that refer to this Condition.
