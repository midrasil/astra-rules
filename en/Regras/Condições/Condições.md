# Conditions

A **Condition** is a state that affects a character during a Scene, imposing mechanical effects while it stays active.

Conditions are applied by Actions, Maneuvers, Capabilities or other rules. They are all gathered here as the single core reference.

## Conditions

- [Prone](Ca%C3%ADdo.md) — on the ground; penalizes their own rolls and changes Attacks made against them.
- [Grappled](Agarrado.md) — held by another character; no movement.
- [Debilitated](Debilitado.md) — Health track full.
- [Incapacitated](Incapacitado.md) — whole Health track aggravated; prone, unable to act or respond.
- [Slowed](Lento.md) — reduced Movement until the next Turn of whoever applied it.
- [Shaken](Abalado.md) — penalty on the next Attack.
- [Poisoned](Envenenado.md) — penalty on Physical rolls.
- [Paralyzed](Paralisado.md) — no Action and no Movement.
- [Stunned](Atordoado.md) — no Action.
- [Fear](Medo.md) — stacking penalty on every Pool.
- [Aiming](Mirando.md) — stacks the bonus of the Aim Maneuver for the next ranged Attack.
- [Read](Lido.md) — analyzed by another character; no effect of its own, used by Capabilities.
- [Challenged](Desafiado.md) — provoked by a Duelist; penalizes Confronts started against other creatures.

## Relations

Conditions are usually applied by [Maneuvers](../Cenas%20e%20Conflitos/A%C3%A7%C3%B5es/Manobras.md) and other effects during [Scenes and Conflicts](../Cenas%20e%20Conflitos/Cenas%20e%20Conflitos.md).
