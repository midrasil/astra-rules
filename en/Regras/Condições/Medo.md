# Fear

**Fear** is a stacking Condition. Each stack imposes:

> **-1 die on all of the character's Dice Pools**

At the **end of each Turn** of the affected character, they lose 1 stack of Fear.

Fear is applied, for example, by the **Terrifying** NPC Capability (see [NPC Capabilities](../Capacidades/Capacidades%20de%20NPC.md)).
