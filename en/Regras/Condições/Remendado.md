# Patched

A **Patched** creature has received a [Patch Up](../Compet%C3%AAncias/Socorrista.md#level-1--patch-up) and has a number of **eased points**. Until the end of the Scene, each eased point changes its [Health](../Personagens/Vida.md) damage:

- one point of aggravated damage becomes superficial;
- if it has no aggravated damage, one point of superficial damage is erased.

Note how many points were converted and how many were erased. An [Incapacitated](Incapacitado.md) creature that is Patched stops being Incapacitated, because its track now has superficial damage.

## End of the Scene

At the end of the Scene, the eased damage comes back:

- the creature takes superficial damage equal to the erased points;
- then, a number of points of superficial damage equal to the converted points becomes aggravated.

This follows the [Full Health Track](../Personagens/Vida.md#full-health-track) rules and can leave the creature Incapacitated, but this damage never kills it.

Then the Condition ends and the creature becomes [Impaired](Prejudicado.md), with **1 stack for each eased point**.
