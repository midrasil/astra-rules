# Debilitated

A character becomes **Debilitated** when their [Health](../Personagens/Vida.md) track is full, with any combination of superficial and aggravated damage.

While Debilitated, they suffer a **-2 dice** penalty on any roll that uses a [Physical Attribute](../Personagens/Atributos.md#categories).
