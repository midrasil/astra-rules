# Unsteady

An **Unsteady** character loses control of their body now and then. At the end of each of their Turns, roll **1 die**: with no Success, they have no Movement on their next Turn.

The Condition lasts as long as its source says. Unsteady can be applied by [Drugs](../Equipamentos/Consum%C3%ADveis.md#drugs), Capabilities or other effects that refer to this Condition.
