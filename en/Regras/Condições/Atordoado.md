# Stunned

A **Stunned** character cannot use their **Action**, but can still use their Movement and respond to Confronts.

The condition lasts **1 Turn**. When it ends, the character is immune to being Stunned again for **1 Turn**.

Stunned can be applied by NPC traits, Capabilities or other effects that refer to this Condition, such as the **Stunning** trait (see [NPC Traits](../Criaturas/Caracter%C3%ADsticas%20de%20NPC.md)).
