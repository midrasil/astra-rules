# Poisoned

A **Poisoned** character gets:

> **-2 dice on rolls that use a Physical Attribute**

The condition lasts the number of Turns the source sets. With the **Venomous** trait (see [NPC Traits](../Criaturas/Caracter%C3%ADsticas%20de%20NPC.md)), that number equals the poison's Potency.

Poisoned can be applied by NPC traits, Capabilities, items or other effects that refer to this Condition.
