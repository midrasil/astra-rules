# Bleeding

**Bleeding** is a stacking Condition. At the **end of each Turn** of the affected character, they take:

> **superficial damage equal to the number of stacks**

After the damage, they lose 1 stack of Bleeding.

This damage has no Damage Type: Absorption, [Resistances and Vulnerabilities](../Resist%C3%AAncia%20e%20Vulnerabilidade/Resist%C3%AAncia%20e%20Vulnerabilidade.md) do not apply to it. With a full [Health](../Personagens/Vida.md) track, it converts superficial damage into aggravated, like any other damage.

## Staunching

An adjacent character, or the affected one, can use their **Action** to staunch the bleeding and test **Cunning + Healing**. Each Success removes **1 stack** of Bleeding.
