# Aiming

An **Aiming** character has used the [Aim](../Cenas%20e%20Conflitos/A%C3%A7%C3%B5es/Manobras.md#aim) Maneuver and is focusing on the target. Each stack gives:

> **+2 dice on the next ranged Attack**

The benefit applies whether the Attack uses Shooting, Throwing or another Attribute set by a rule.

The character holds at most a number of stacks equal to their **Cunning**.

All stacks are lost if, before attacking, the character:

- uses their Movement after Aiming;
- is moved by force;
- takes damage;
- becomes [Prone](Ca%C3%ADdo.md).

Making the Attack also ends the Aim, unless a Capability expressly says otherwise.

The Condition also ends at the end of combat.
