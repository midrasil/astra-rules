# Challenged

A Challenged character was provoked by a Duelist to remain in combat against them.

The Condition belongs to the Duelist who applied it. The Challenged character takes **-3 dice** when starting a Confront against any other creature.

A character Challenged by more than one Duelist takes the **-3 dice** only once and takes no penalty when starting a Confront against any of them.

Challenged ends at the end of the Scene, when the Duelist who applied the Condition starts a Confront against another creature, or when that Duelist becomes [Incapacitated](Incapacitado.md) or leaves the Scene.
