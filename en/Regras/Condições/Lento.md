# Slowed

A **Slowed** character has their Movement reduced by:

> **-2 Movement**

The condition lasts until the start of the next Turn of whoever applied it and then ends on its own. Applying Slowed again does not stack the penalty: it only renews the duration.

Slowed can be applied by Capabilities, Masteries of weapons or other effects that refer to this Condition.
