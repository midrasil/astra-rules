# Incapacitated

A character becomes **Incapacitated** when their whole [Health](../Personagens/Vida.md) track is filled with aggravated damage.

While Incapacitated, the character:

- is [Prone](Ca%C3%ADdo.md);
- cannot take Actions or Movement;
- cannot respond to Confronts and is a [target without a response](../Cenas%20e%20Conflitos/A%C3%A7%C3%B5es/Confrontar.md#target-without-a-response).

The Condition ends when at least 1 point of aggravated Health damage is converted into superficial, usually through [Treatment](../Personagens/Vida.md#treatment).

The fate of an Incapacitated character who keeps taking damage is up to the Narrator.
