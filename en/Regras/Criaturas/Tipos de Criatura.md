# Creature Types

The **Creature Type** classifies the nature of a character or creature. The default is **Humanoid**.

The starting list of types is:

- **Humanoid**
- **Beast**
- **Monstrosity**
- **Undead**
- **Construct**
- **Elemental**
- **Spirit**
- **Aberration**
- **Plant**
- **Dragon**

A creature can also be **Typeless** when no category applies.

For now, Creature Type **has no mechanical effect**. It serves as a reference for the Narrator and as a basis for future rules, such as Capabilities, Resistances or effects that affect only certain types. Settings and modules can add new types.
